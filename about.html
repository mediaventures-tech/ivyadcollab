Who We Are

Redefining how brands reach the next generation

We aggregate six premier university newspapers into a single, streamlined sales channel. By centralizing pricing, sponsor outreach, campaign execution, and reporting, we eliminate the friction national advertisers face when trying to reach high-value college demographics across multiple top-tier institutions.

No more negotiating with six different publications. No fragmented reporting. Just one deal — and access to the most engaged student audiences in the country.
